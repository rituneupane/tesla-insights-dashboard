# Notes

I started this because I already knew the Model 3 production story (vertical integration, automation, the messy ramp). I wanted numbers on what happened after that.

## Mix

Almost all of the volume is Model 3 and Model Y. S, X, and Cybertruck stay small. If I were comparing Tesla to other EVs, I would start with 3/Y, not the expensive models.

## Scale, then slower growth

The big jump is 2020–2023. After that, deliveries flattened and then dropped in 2025. Price cuts helped keep volume up for a while, but they did not keep the 2021–2022 growth going.

Q1 2025 looked especially weak. That lines up with the Model Y refresh / factory changeover, so I would not treat one quarter as the whole story. The full-year number still came in below 2023.

## Margin

Auto gross margin was strongest around 2021 (~29%). It is closer to the high teens now. Volume went up; selling prices came down faster than that helped costs.

## Energy

Energy is the part that is still growing. Auto is still most of the company, but energy going from ~$2B to ~$12.8B in five years is the second story I would put on a slide.

## What I would not claim

- I did not model Tesla's stock.
- I did not build a factory-by-factory capacity file.
- Competitor unit sales are not in this dataset.
