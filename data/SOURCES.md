# Sources

Numbers come from Tesla's public filings:

- [Tesla IR](https://ir.tesla.com/): quarterly production and delivery releases
- Tesla 10-K on SEC EDGAR (CIK 0001318605): revenue by segment, automotive gross margin, operating margin

## Company tab and 2026 update (fetched October 24, 2026)

- GM and Ford Form 10-K FY2025 (SEC XBRL): revenue $185.0B and $187.3B, used for the peer revenue view.

## Caveats

- Total deliveries and production, 2016 to 2025: IR totals
- Model 3/Y vs other: official from 2020 on; 2017 to 2019 mix is estimated because Tesla reports 3/Y together later
- Revenue and margins: 10-K / earnings updates
- Quarterly deliveries in this file: 2023 Q1 through 2026 Q3
