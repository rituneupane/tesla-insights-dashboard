# Sources

Numbers come from Tesla's public filings:

- [Tesla IR](https://ir.tesla.com/) — quarterly production and delivery releases
- Tesla 10-K on SEC EDGAR (CIK 0001318605) — revenue by segment, automotive gross margin, operating margin

## Caveats

- Total deliveries and production, 2016–2025: IR totals
- Model 3/Y vs other: official from 2020 on; 2017–2019 mix is estimated because Tesla reports 3/Y together later
- Revenue and margins: 10-K / earnings updates
- Quarterly deliveries in this file: 2023 Q1 through 2025 Q4

If a number here disagrees with a later Tesla filing, use Tesla's number.
